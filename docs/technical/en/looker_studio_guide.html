Google Looker Studio BI Dashboard Guide

Comprehensive blueprint for building an enterprise-grade, zero-licensing cost executive revenue and customer intelligence cockpit connected directly to BigQuery Gold dimensional models.

1. 🔌 Connecting BigQuery Gold Marts in Looker Studio

Follow these steps to establish high-speed, direct connections from Google Looker Studio into your serverless BigQuery data lakehouse:

  1. Navigate to Google Looker Studio ↗ and click Create ➔ Data Source.
  2. Select the official BigQuery native connector:
    • Project: Select your GCP Project ID (de-consulting-508822).
    • Dataset: Select platzi_gold.
    • Recommended Tables to Connect:
      • gold_daily_sales_kpi — Core sales volume, GMV, Net Revenue & AOV metrics.
      • gold_customer_ltv — Customer lifetime value, RFM tiers & loyalty cohorts.
      • gold_product_performance — Product catalog margins, velocity & refund rates.
Open Google Looker Studio ↗

2. 🎨 3-Page Executive Visual Blueprint

Page 1: Executive Revenue & Order Trends

  • Top KPI Scorecards: GMV (Gross Merchandise Value), Net Revenue (Actual cash intake, completed orders only), AOV (Average Order Value), Completed Orders vs. Cancellation Rate (%).
  • Main Dual-Axis Time Series Chart:
    • Dimension: order_date (Date)
    • Metric 1 (Bars): gmv (Light Blue)
    • Metric 2 (Line): net_revenue (Dark Blue / Emerald Green)
    • Date Range Control: Quick filter for Last 30 / 60 / 90 Days.

Page 2: Customer Cohorts & Lifetime Value (LTV)

  • Customer Tier Distribution (Donut Chart):
    • Dimension: customer_tier (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze)
    • Metric: Customer Count (Record Count) & lifetime_net_revenue.
  • High-Value VIP Customer Leaderboard (Table):
    • Dimensions: customer_id, customer_name, customer_tier, first_order_date, last_order_date.
    • Metrics: completed_orders, lifetime_net_revenue.
    • (Security Note: Customer PII such as email/phone is cryptographically salted & SHA-256 masked at Silver layer).

Page 3: Merchandising & Product Performance

  • Top Revenue Generators (Bar Chart):
    • Dimension: product_title
    • Metric: completed_sales_amount (Sorted Descending).
  • Category Breakdown (Treemap):
    • Dimension: category_name | Metric: units_sold.
  • Refund & Return Rate Watchlist (Table):
    • Highlights SKUs with elevated refunded_units for early supply chain & quality warnings.

3. ⚡ FinOps & Query Performance Optimization

  • Zero-Slot Query Costs: Queries running against platzi_gold benefit from pre-aggregated dbt models, scanning only kilobytes of data per refresh instead of full-table scans.
  • BI Engine Acceleration: BigQuery BI Engine caches memory-optimized execution plans for Looker Studio, delivering sub-second interactive filtering.
  • Partition Pruning: Filter orders using order_date partition boundaries to eliminate 70%~90% of raw data scans.
